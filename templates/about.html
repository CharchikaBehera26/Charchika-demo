OUR PURPOSE

Connecting craft, culture & community

SrujanSetu is an educational project concept designed to showcase Odisha’s traditional crafts and the people who keep them thriving.

Our vision

To create a welcoming digital bridge between artisans, self-help groups, learners and people who value handmade traditions.

What this demo includes

Craft discovery, artisan profiles, a sample marketplace, craft stories, scheme information, a visit planner, community notes and a rule-based assistant.

Learning project notice

All sample profiles, product listings, counts and scheme descriptions are demonstration data. Replace them with verified information before any real-world launch.
